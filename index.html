Doctrine & source authority

Training aid — source hierarchy

Approved doctrinal basis: FM 3-98 and ATP 3-20.98 for reconnaissance/security; ATP 3-21.90 for mortar employment; ATP 3-01.81 for C-UAS; and ATP 3-09.30 for observed-fire procedures used in CFFT.

Experimental formation baseline: The February 2026 MBCT Handbook is used only to describe the emerging MPC organization and intended integration of Scout, Mortar, Assault, UAS, and effects capabilities. It is an experimentation/training reference, not approved doctrine.

UAS: SRR and MRR are local/formation-specific trainer labels, not doctrinal echelons. ATP 3-90.51 is distribution-restricted; this public trainer intentionally uses only high-level planning concepts and does not reproduce restricted procedures.

Reports: BLUE 1 / BLUE 3 / BLUE 7 / BLUE 9 are local/reference-card training formats in this build, not asserted Army-wide report standards. Current higher-HQ instructions and unit SOP govern.

Scoring: Scores are a leader-development heuristic, not an official Army evaluation, certification, or doctrinal rating. Current publications, orders, ROE, commander guidance, safety requirements, and unit SOPs always govern execution.

How the decision-performance indicators are measured
MISSION ALIGNMENTDid the decision directly support the stated mission / supported commander requirement?
INFORMATION DISCIPLINEDid the leader distinguish observation, assessment, confidence, gaps, and stale information?
TIMELINESSDid the decision or report arrive before the relevant decision window closed?
RISK & OPTION PRESERVATIONDid the action retain freedom of maneuver, endurance, survivability, or follow-on options?
SYNCHRONIZATIONWere collection, movement, effects, reporting, and handover sequenced to support each other?
ADAPTATIONDid the leader adjust to new information, enemy action, degradation, or changed guidance?
COMMUNICATIONDid the selected action communicate the required information at the point it mattered? Free-text quality still requires leader/facilitator review.

These are automated leader-development indicators derived from scenario decision scores and observable trainer behaviors. They are not validated psychometric measures and should not be used as standalone certification or personnel evaluation.
